## Metadata (App Store Connect)

- [ ] Subtitle/description: «цифровой друг», «эмоциональная поддержка», «самопомощь»
- [ ] **Нет:** cure, treat depression, therapy, psychologist replacement, diagnosis
- [ ] Screenshots без PHQ scores как «диагноз»
- [ ] Age rating согласован с child 8–12 (2 Hub buttons only)

## In-app (before first wellness session)

- [ ] `wellness_consent_*` shown (see `legal_disclaimers.md`)
- [ ] Screening labeled «скрининг», not «диагноз»
- [ ] L3 crisis → 112 copy (existing `companion_ethics`)

## Health claims we do NOT make

- [ ] No PPG / camera stress measurement
- [ ] No «лечит травму/депрессию»
- [ ] No live human coaches in-app

ALADDIN provides optional self-help mood support inside a family safety app. Content is educational; crisis paths direct users to emergency services. Not a medical device.
