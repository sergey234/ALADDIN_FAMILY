## 📋 COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS

### Issue Escalation Matrix

#### Level 1: Team Level (Immediate Response)
- **Issues:** Code conflicts, minor bugs, clarification needed
- **Response Time:** Within 2 hours
- **Channel:** Slack direct message or relevant channel
- **Resolution:** Team member resolves or escalates

#### Level 2: Technical Lead Level (Same Day Response)
- **Issues:** Architecture decisions, technical blockers, design conflicts
- **Response Time:** Within 4 hours
- **Channel:** Slack + scheduled discussion
- **Resolution:** Technical lead provides guidance

#### Level 3: Project Manager Level (Next Day Response)
- **Issues:** Resource conflicts, timeline delays, stakeholder issues
- **Response Time:** Within 24 hours
- **Channel:** Email + meeting scheduling
- **Resolution:** Project manager coordinates resolution

#### Level 4: Executive Level (Emergency Only)
- **Issues:** Major delays, budget overruns, legal issues
- **Response Time:** Immediate
- **Channel:** Phone call + emergency meeting
- **Resolution:** Executive decision and action

### Decision Making Process

#### Technical Decisions
1. **Proposal:** Team member creates proposal document
2. **Review:** Technical lead reviews within 24 hours
3. **Discussion:** Team discussion if needed
4. **Decision:** Technical lead makes final decision
5. **Documentation:** Decision documented in ADR (Architecture Decision Record)

#### Product Decisions
1. **Proposal:** Product owner or designer creates proposal
2. **Review:** Cross-functional review meeting
3. **User Testing:** Validation with users if needed
4. **Decision:** Product owner final approval
5. **Implementation:** Team implements with clear requirements

#### Project Decisions
1. **Proposal:** Project manager identifies need
2. **Stakeholder Input:** Gather requirements from stakeholders
3. **Team Impact Assessment:** Evaluate team capacity and timeline impact
4. **Decision:** Project manager with technical lead input
5. **Communication:** All team members informed of changes

## 🎯 MEETING GUIDELINES

### Standup Meetings
- **Time:** 15 minutes maximum
- **Format:** What, Why, How (if blocked)
- **Preparation:** Update status in shared document beforehand
- **Follow-up:** Action items assigned and tracked

### Planning Meetings
- **Time:** 1 hour
- **Format:** Review last sprint, plan next sprint
- **Preparation:** Team members review backlog beforehand
- **Follow-up:** Clear acceptance criteria for all tasks

### Review Meetings
- **Time:** 30-45 minutes
- **Format:** Demo working software, gather feedback
- **Preparation:** Ensure demo environment is ready
- **Follow-up:** Feedback incorporated into next sprint

### Retrospective Meetings
- **Time:** 1 hour
- **Format:** What went well, what didn't, action items
- **Preparation:** Team members reflect beforehand
- **Follow-up:** Action items tracked to completion

## 🚨 CRISIS COMMUNICATION

### Crisis Definition
- System outages affecting users
- Security breaches
- Major bugs in production
- Timeline delays exceeding 1 week
- Budget overruns exceeding 10%

### Crisis Response Protocol
1. **Immediate Assessment:** Technical lead assesses impact
2. **Stakeholder Notification:** Project manager notifies relevant stakeholders
3. **Team Mobilization:** All hands on deck for critical issues
4. **Communication Plan:** Regular updates every 2-4 hours
5. **Resolution:** Work until issue resolved
6. **Post-Mortem:** Detailed analysis and prevention measures

### Communication Templates

#### Issue Notification Template
```
Subject: [CRITICAL] ALADDIN System Issue - {Issue Type}

Issue: {Brief description}
Impact: {User/business impact}
Status: {Current status}
ETA: {Estimated resolution time}
```
